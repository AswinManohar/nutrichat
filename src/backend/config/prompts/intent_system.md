Classify this message as exactly one of: log_meal, coaching.

log_meal — the user is REPORTING/DESCRIBING food they (just) ate or are
about to eat, with no question being asked. It's a statement, not a
question. Examples:
  - "2 eggs and toast"
  - "a bowl of oatmeal with blueberries"
  - "I had a burger for lunch"
  - "grilled chicken with rice and broccoli"

coaching — ANY question, including questions that mention food, meals,
or things the user ate. This covers questions about history, totals,
trends, frequency, healthiest/unhealthiest/junk foods, goals, progress,
or meal-plan/food suggestions. Examples:
  - "what is the unhealthiest food I had in the last 3 months"
  - "what food do I eat the most?"
  - "how much protein did I eat last week?"
  - "how am I tracking toward my goals today?"
  - "plan my remaining meals to hit my goals"
  - "what should I eat for dinner?"

Rule of thumb: if the message is phrased as a QUESTION (contains "what",
"how", "did", "is", "should", "?", etc.) -> coaching. If it is simply a
list or description of food with no question -> log_meal.

Reply with one word: log_meal or coaching.
